FAQ

How do I add a new plant?

Tap the + button on the dashboard. Follow the 4-step wizard to set the name, species, environment, and last care date.

How do adaptive schedules work?

Verdura tracks when you actually water and care for your plants, then adjusts future reminders to match your real routine rather than a fixed timer.

Is my data safe?

All data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account required.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases". Your premium access will be restored automatically.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Settings and use the export option to save your plant data as a backup file.
